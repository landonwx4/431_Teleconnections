## Supplemental Figures

Included in this folder are three notebooks to generate valuable supplemental figures, as well as pre-generated figures for two important types of data.

The first, in the folder marked "Global Correlations", shows the most highly coorelated teleconnection for each region and variable. This is meant to highlight at a glance which teleconnection indicies could be the most useful when attempting to predict temperature and precipitation anomalies.

The second set of pre-generated figures, in the folder marked "Spaghetti Plots", shows the correlation coefficient (Pearson r-value)
